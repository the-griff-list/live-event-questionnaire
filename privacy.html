Privacy Notice

Anonymous Live Event Questionnaire — PEI
Created by Griffen Arsenault · Last updated November 12, 2026

1. Purpose

This voluntary questionnaire gathers experiences, opinions, and suggestions about the live music community in Prince Edward Island. Responses may be reviewed to understand community needs, identify challenges, and inform summaries or recommendations about PEI's live music scene.

2. Information collected

Answers may include your role in the live music community, attendance and work experiences, opinions about the local music scene, selected challenges, and optional written feedback. You do not need an account to participate, and you may leave the contact fields blank.

Please do not include sensitive personal information or details that identify you or another person in the optional written-feedback field.

3. Optional contact information

You may optionally provide your name, email address, or phone number if you want to be contacted. These details are stored in a separate database table from the questionnaire answers, but an internal response identifier links them to the corresponding submission so a follow-up can be made. Contact details are not required to participate.

4. Anonymous participation and technical information

The questionnaire does not require an account or ask you to identify yourself. However, free-text answers may identify someone if identifying details are included. The service also processes limited technical information needed to deliver the website, protect the submission endpoint, and prevent automated abuse.

5. Service providers and location of storage

The website is delivered through Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare Turnstile provides an automated-abuse check and may process browser, device, and connection information for security purposes. Questionnaire responses and optional contact details are stored using Supabase. The Supabase project is hosted in the United States (Oregon), so information may be processed or stored outside Canada.

6. How information is used and shared

Information is used to analyze experiences and needs in PEI's live music community and to prepare aggregated summaries, reports, or recommendations. Responses are not intended to be published as identifiable personal profiles. Access to stored information is restricted through database security controls and authorized administrative access. Information is not sold.

7. Retention

There is not currently a published fixed retention period for questionnaire responses or optional contact details. The administrator should review stored information periodically and delete contact details when follow-up is complete and information when it is no longer needed for the questionnaire's stated purposes. A specific retention schedule should be established and this notice updated when one is adopted.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

Participation is voluntary. You may skip optional questions and leave contact details blank. If you supplied contact information and want to ask about its removal, contact the questionnaire administrator through the contact method provided by the person or organization that shared this questionnaire with you.

Because responses are not associated with participant accounts or names, the administrator may be unable to locate a particular anonymous response unless you can provide enough information to identify it. Do not send sensitive personal information in an ordinary email or public message.

9. Questions and updates

For questions about this notice, contact the questionnaire administrator through the contact method provided by the person or organization that shared this questionnaire. This notice may be updated if the questionnaire's data practices or service providers change. The current version will be published on this page.
